The problem

Most claims are lost before anyone argues them

01 —

The clock starts before anyone looks.

Under FIDIC you have 28 days from the day you became aware of an event — or should have. Miss it and the right never arises: in a 2023 Dubai case, 306 days of employer delay earned the contractor zero days of extension.[2]

ContractClauseWhat must happenDeadlineIf it is missed
FIDIC 201718.2Notice of an Exceptional Event14 days from awarenessExcused only from the date the notice is received
FIDIC 201720.2.1Notice of Claim for time or money28 days from awarenessThe other party is discharged, subject to 20.2.2 and 20.2.5
FIDIC 201720.2.2Engineer objects to a late Notice, with reasons14 days from receiving itThe Notice is deemed valid
FIDIC 201720.2.4Fully detailed Claim, with legal basis and records84 days from awareness, or as agreedThe Notice lapses if the legal basis is missing
FIDIC 199919.2Notice of Force Majeure14 days from awarenessRelief may be lost

About

We build the record that decides who gets paid

Construction contracts turn events into time and money, but only for the party that notices — in writing, in time. The evidence is almost always on file: a diary line, an instruction, a supplier’s email. Nobody reads all of it as it is written.

Lavinora does. We compile your contract once, read every record as it lands, and keep a living Entitlement Ledger: what happened, what it entitles you to, and the date that right expires.

We don’t replace your commercial team or your claims consultant. We make sure there is still a claim when they need one — and that the side with the better record is yours.

Why I’m building Lavinora

I grew up inside my family’s industrial electrical distribution business, one rung down the contractor supply chain. When I started working in it, most of my time went to documents — invoices, spreadsheets, contracts, correspondence. I learnt how much of a company’s money is decided by paper that nobody has time to read properly.

That ceiling is why I started building AI systems. Construction claims are the sharpest version of the problem I know: the facts are on file, the deadline is written in the contract, and a right worth millions can expire in 28 days because nobody connected the two. Lavinora connects them, on the day the record is written.

We are looking for our first design partners in the Gulf and India. If you run commercial on a FIDIC or NEC project and have ever lost a claim to a time bar, I’d like to hear how it happened.
