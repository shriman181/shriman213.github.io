# Lavinora

**Win on the merits, not the paperwork.**

Lavinora reads the construction project record as it is written — site diaries, Engineer's instructions, RFIs, minutes, correspondence and programme updates. It recognises the events that entitle a contractor to time or money under FIDIC and NEC contracts, starts the contractual clock, and drafts the notice for a named person to sign before the time bar closes.

## What Lavinora does

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Day-1 Register** | Compiles the contract into every notice duty: trigger, deadline, form and recipient, with Particular Conditions applied. |
| **Event recognition** | Reads each record as it lands, in English and Arabic, and flags events that carry entitlement, citing the earliest record. |
| **The Clock** | Computes time bars deterministically from the contract, for example FIDIC 2017 Sub-Clauses 18.2, 20.2.1 and 20.2.4, and NEC4 clause 61.3. |
| **Notice drafting** | Drafts notices that meet the contract's form rules. Nothing is sent automatically: an authorised person signs every notice. |
| **Impact analysis** | Places events into the accepted programme (Primavera P6 or MS Project) to show the effect on completion. |
| **Entitlement Ledger** | Keeps every event, notice and outcome across a project portfolio. |

Contracts supported: FIDIC 1999 and 2017 (Red, Yellow, Silver) and NEC3 and NEC4 ECC, plus statutory notice duties under the UAE Civil Transactions Law. Lavinora is built first for contractors and Engineers in the GCC and India.

The website includes a free **notice clock**: choose the contract and event, enter the date you became aware, and it lists every deadline and exports them as a calendar file. [Try it](https://lavinora.cloud/#library).
